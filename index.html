About

Promise Omiyedun

Promise Omiyedun is a Nigerian technology creator and the Founder and CEO of X-ZITH TECHNOLOGY. His work focuses on building digital platforms, educational technology products, artificial intelligence projects and software designed to provide useful solutions for people.

Through X-ZITH TECHNOLOGY, Promise is developing projects across education, artificial intelligence, communication and digital technology.

His projects include X-ZITH Learning Hub, Fixto AI and Zexap Chat.

His goal is to continue learning, creating and developing technology that can grow from useful ideas into platforms used by people around the world.
